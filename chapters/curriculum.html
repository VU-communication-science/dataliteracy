Curriculum & Learning Trajectories

A visual roadmap of methods competencies across the Communication Science program

Welcome to the curriculum overview for Data Literacy at VU Amsterdam.

Communication science research relies on a progressive sequence of skills—from foundational data hygiene and reproducible workflows to psychometrics, hypothesis testing, and advanced regression modeling.

Below is the Methods Skill Tree. Every module clearly defines its prerequisites (what starting knowledge is assumed) and what it unlocks next.

  • Hover over any module to inspect its prerequisites, description, and downstream applications.
  • Click any module to jump directly to its lesson.

🗺️ The Methods Skill Tree

💡 How to Use the Skill Tree

  • No false prerequisites: You do not need to complete Data Cleaning or Factor Analysis before jumping into a \(t\)-test or Correlation. Follow the vertical paths straight down from Working with R \(\rightarrow\) Data Frames \(\rightarrow\) Summarizing \(\rightarrow\) Tests.
  • Specialized tracks when needed:
    • Conducting a survey? Unlock Measurement & Scales (Latent Variables \(\rightarrow\) Factor Analysis \(\rightarrow\) Scale Construction).
    • Cleaning messy wild data? Unlock Data Cleaning.
    • Testing experimental manipulations? Follow T-Test \(\rightarrow\) ANOVA \(\rightarrow\) ANCOVA.
    • Testing theoretical mechanisms? Follow Correlation \(\rightarrow\) Linear Regression \(\rightarrow\) Controlling \(\rightarrow\) Mediation.
