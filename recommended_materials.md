# Curated Recommended Materials & Resources

This document provides a standardized reference library of recommended books, interactive tools, videos, and articles that enrich the Data Literacy methods curriculum.

---

## 1. Companion Textbooks

- **[R for Communication Science](https://r4css.vanatteveldt.com/)** *(Wouter van Atteveldt et al.)*
  - *Focus*: Comprehensive guide to computational communication science in R, with in-depth coverage of web scraping, APIs, automated content analysis, and advanced text classification.
  - *Role*: The primary companion for students seeking deep computational tutorials beyond this canon book.
- **[R for Data Science (2e)](https://r4ds.hadley.nz/)** *(Hadley Wickham, Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel, & Garrett Grolemund)*
  - *Focus*: The definitive guide to tidyverse principles, data import, transformation, and visualization.

## 2. Conceptual Video Explanations

### 3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)
Exceptional visual and mathematical intuitions for core statistical and calculus concepts:
- **[Central Limit Theorem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zeJD6dqJ5lo)**: Why do sums and averages of random variables always take the bell curve shape?
- **[Bayes' Theorem Visualized](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HZGCoVF3YvM)**: Intuitive geometric proof of conditional probability and updating prior beliefs.
- **[What is a Probability Distribution?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZA4JkHKZM50)**: Visualizing continuous probability density functions.

### StatQuest with Josh Starmer
Clear, jargon-free step-by-step visual breakdowns of statistical tests:
- **[Hypothesis Testing and P-values Clearly Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vemZtEM63GY)**
- **[T-Tests, Degrees of Freedom, and Standard Error](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Pd3dc1GcHc)**
- **[Linear Models & R-squared](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2AQKmw14mHM)**
- **[Logistic Regression Details](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIYKR4sgzI8)**

## 3. Interactive Web Simulators & Tools

- **[Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/)** *(Brown University)*
  - A visual introduction to probability and statistics through interactive browser simulations (compound probability, distributions, statistical inference, Bayesian thinking, regression).
- **[Guess the Correlation](http://guessthecorrelation.com/)**
  - An interactive game that trains intuition for scatterplots and Pearson's $r$.
- **[DAGitty](https://dagitty.net/)**
  - Interactive browser tool for creating Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs), finding minimal adjustment sets, and identifying confounding paths.
- **[Kristoffer Magnusson's Interactive Statistics Visualizations](https://rpsychologist.com/viz/)**
  - Interactively visualize Cohen's $d$, statistical power, and confidence intervals by dragging sliders.

## 4. Methodological Reading & Open Science

- **Lakens, D. (2022)**. *Improving Your Statistical Inferences*. Online open-access book on effect sizes, power analysis, and error control.
- **Wagenmakers, E. J. et al. (2018)**. *An Agenda for Purely Confirmatory Research*. Perspectives on Psychological Science.
- **Rohrer, J. M. (2018)**. *Thinking Clearly About Correlation and Causation: Graphical Causal Models for Observational Data*. Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science.
